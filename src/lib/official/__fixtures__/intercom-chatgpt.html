November 23, 2026

GPT-6.2, now in the chat tab

We’re rolling out a version of GPT-6.2, optimized for the chat tab, for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise tiers. It’s better at handling more complex requests that involve following detailed instructions or finding information online. If you’re comparing hotels for a weekend trip, it can help you weigh trade-offs like price and location and recommend the one that best fits your priorities.

It’s also better at knowing when to follow up. For example, if you ask whether something is on sale, ChatGPT can offer to let you know when the price drops.

Better at understanding images and following instructions

GPT‑6.2 is better at answering questions about the images you share. In our tests in the Chat tab, it scored 71.7% on image understanding, up from 47.4% for GPT‑6.

On especially challenging prompts, GPT-6.2 followed instructions 78.1% of the time, up from 62.5% for GPT-6.

At the lowest reasoning setting we tested, GPT‑6.2 scored 65% on an internal evaluation of expert-level tasks, up from 52% for GPT‑6.

GPT‑6.2 was also better at finding answers to difficult questions on the web. On an internal version of BrowseComp, it outperformed GPT‑6 at all four reasoning settings. At Extra high, GPT‑6.2 scored about 10 percentage points higher (64.5% vs. 54.4%).

Deploying GPT-6.2 safely

GPT‑6.2 shows significantly improved performance on our teen safety evaluations, with the largest gains in emotional reliance, disordered eating, and age-restricted goods, services, and dangerous activities. In evaluations of policy compliance in challenging conversations, responses met our safety policies 91–94% of the time, up from 77–80% for GPT‑6. Read more in our system card. 

Availability

GPT‑6.2 starts rolling out globally to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise tiers today in the Chat tab. Enterprise availability depends on workplace admin settings. GPT‑6.2 in ChatGPT is powered by a version of GPT‑6.2 Sol for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise tiers, and is tuned for everyday conversation. This update applies to the Chat experience in ChatGPT. The models powering Work and Codex are not changing as part of this release.

November 20, 2026

ChatGPT Space is rolling out to Plus

Starting today, ChatGPT Space is rolling out globally to Plus users ages 18 and older. In Space, you can create interactive pages and presentations with ChatGPT, then invite other people with Space access to build on them with you. You can also set pages and slides to automatically update based on your connected apps and tools.

Try making a to-do list that updates itself, a study guide with a 3D model you can explore, or a project plan that stays current with decisions from Slack. Tag ChatGPT in a comment to ask for changes. If you have a dot, you can invite it to collaborate, too.

To open a shared page or presentation, you need permission to view it and must be signed in to a Plus, Pro, Business, or Enterprise account with Space access. People on Free can’t open shared pages or presentations. Public links aren’t available.

To get started, open Space in the sidebar. It replaces Library and is your new home for pages, slides, images, and uploaded files.

Use Space on the web, desktop app, and iOS and Android to create and edit pages. 

November 19, 2026

New mobile experience for ChatGPT

We’re rolling out a new experience for the ChatGPT mobile app that makes it easier to get to your favorite features and discover more of what you can do. Your dot has its own tab, and you can pin favorites like Codex, Projects, and Images to the tab bar.

We’re starting with ChatGPT Pro users who are 18 or older in regions where dots are available. This rollout excludes the European Economic Area (including the EU), the UK, Switzerland, and Korea. A small group of eligible Plus users will also see the new experience.

Some eligible Pro users will see the new experience by default. If New ChatGPT design appears in Settings, you can turn it on or off there.

Learn more about the new experience in the ChatGPT mobile app.

Health on Android, desktop, dots and sites

Starting today, Health is rolling out to more of the places you use ChatGPT, including the desktop app, dots, sites, and Android, with support for Health Connect. 

  • Connect your health and fitness information with Android Health Connect to explore patterns in your sleep, activity, and more.

  • Get proactive suggestions from your dot, like another time for a workout when a meeting gets in the way or a reminder about preventive care options based on your connected medical records. 

  • Use the desktop app to build a site to track your marathon training, with your runs and progress toward your training plan in one place.

To get started, update to the latest ChatGPT app and select Health in the sidebar. If it isn’t listed, find Health in the Explore menu. This update is rolling out gradually, so it may not be available for your account yet.

Health is available to Free, Go, Plus, and Pro users in the U.S. Dots are currently available to Pro users. See: Getting started with your dot.

Learn more about Health in ChatGPT.

November 18, 2026

Live Dictation in ChatGPT

Dictation in ChatGPT is getting faster and more reliable.

With Live Dictation, your speech turns into text as you talk, so you spend less time waiting for your message to finish transcribing. Tap or click on the waveform to show or hide text as you go. We’ve also removed the 10-minute recording limit.

Live Dictation is rolling out globally to all ChatGPT plans on iOS, Android, web, and the desktop app.

Learn more about voice dictation.

Accounting workflows in ChatGPT

The Accounting plugin helps you reconcile transactions, draft invoice entries, and prepare month-end reports from your records and templates.

Open the ChatGPT desktop app for macOS or Windows, set up computer use, and open the Accounting plugin listing. Select Install plugin if it’s available for your plan and workspace. In Enterprise workspaces, an admin must approve Accounting before members can install it.
